Brand & customer strategy

Morphe
Re:Color

Imperial College London · Team project

What if shoppers could replace the shade they use up and keep the palette they already own?

The brief

Our team developed a concept for a customisable, refillable Morphe palette. We focused on beauty shoppers who value self-expression and want to avoid wasting shades.

My contribution

I developed the customer-convenience section of the digital pitch. I used a familiar scenario as the starting point: a favourite shade runs out while the others are still full. I turned that insight into a journey covering discovery, customisation and reordering.

  1. DiscoverWatch a short refill demo.
  2. CustomiseChoose and preview shades.
  3. SaveKeep shade names for later.
  4. RefillFind the compatible pan.

What we made

The team pitch paired a reusable magnetic case with individual refill pans and a digital palette-builder prototype. Our proposed launch journey linked short-form social video to the builder.

media mix

Imperial College London · Team project

How should a brand split £150,000 across TikTok, Google and Instagram?

The brief

Using 200 weeks of spend and sales, our team compared a log–log baseline with adstock models that account for how prior weeks’ spend may affect current sales.

My contribution

I contributed to the R analysis, including the adstock model comparison, chronological holdout evaluation and tests for interactions between channels.

The recommendation

TikTok had the highest estimated elasticity in the selected model, followed by Google and Instagram. We weighted the proposed budget by those estimates.

The recommendation was directional. The data did not capture factors such as promotions, seasonality and competitor activity. The team proposed validating the allocation with controlled tests.

See the modelling approach in R

The script applied a shared adstock decay of 0.01 to chronologically ordered spend, then used an 80/20 time split to compare holdout prediction errors.

adstock <- function(spend, decay = 0.01) {
  as.numeric(stats::filter(spend, decay, method = "recursive"))
}

Project description

We used colour to give each scene in our Yake Tiantian Bang advert a distinct mood. The idea was simple: a lollipop can feel like a different treat depending on which one you pick, where you are, or how you enjoy it. We wanted to show how a small treat can bring a little joy to anyone’s day, whatever the occasion.
